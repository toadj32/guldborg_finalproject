Intended Behavior:

Inspired by Rube Goldberg machines this game involves a constantly moving ball that must go through a perilous path
littered with holes it could fall through, or obstacles that can get in the way and slow its progress. the player must
use blocks and springs and place them accordingly to keep the ball from falling. These items are purchased from the bar
on the right side of the screen, from which the player can click, drag, and drop items. Currency is spawned on the path
and gained when the ball rolls into them.
the Goal is to get the ball to the end of the path as quickly as possible and with as few coins as possible - both of
which will factor into the final score.
the player's high score is tracked and displayed at the bottom left of the screen during gameplay, along with time
elapsed during current run, coins collected; above this is the progress bar that shows proportionally how far the player
is from the finish line.
The ball will periodically have a sparkle explosion around it.

Victoria: The map is meant to be centered around the ball. So I added a cameraPosition offset to insure the ball
is always at the center of the screen. I also added the obstacles and holes and wrote behaviors for the ball to bounce
off and interact with the obstacles in a reasonable way as well as fall into the holes. The components in the sidebar
are meant to be able to be dragged onto the path, not appearing if the user places them off the path. Coins also appear
on the map and disappear as the user collects them. If the user buys a component, they coin counter on the side decreases.

same as the fixed obstacles.
